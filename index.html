About

Hi, I’m Eric. I just graduated from Yale University with a double major in Computer Science and Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology. I work at the intersection of AI and biology, building agents and fine-tuned models for biotech workflows.

Some things I’ve been working on: I’m currently fine-tuning Qwen for target prioritization workflows (Jun 2026 – Present) — I built a 27-tool biomedical RL environment with verifiable rewards via a co-designed 24-dimension expert rubric and an LLM-judge rejection-sampling filter for long-horizon, multi-tool call biotech target prioritization workflows. I distilled a frontier model (Claude) into Qwen3.5-9B via SFT (full fine-tune, DeepSpeed ZeRO-2 CPU-offload, 2×H100), raising the average expert-rubric score from 1.94 to 3.75/5 (vs Claude = 4.36/5) and beating the rubric-prompted base model by 1.7 points (2.05/5), deployed on Modal + Cloud Run.

In the van Dijk Lab at Yale (Sept 2025 – Present), I’ve been enabling chain-of-thought reasoning in C2S models — improving accuracy and enabling reasoning for biological tasks with SFT in C2S Qwen models, biological foundation models capable of finding new variants in cancer. I also spent time building AI SaaS tools for the biotech community (Feb 2026 – Jun 2026), where I secured a pilot with a biotech advisory firm for an AI competitive intelligence SaaS web platform and talked to 20 biotech/investor firms to learn about their pain points.

Previously, I worked on AI agents at Applovin and was building a better Siri with Martin (YC S23). I also achieved MIT Battlecode top 10 and the Most Innovative Breakthrough Award in 2025, and was named a Regeneron STS Top 300 Scholar in 2022.

In my free time, I like to watch films. Hopefully one day AI is good enough that everyone will be able to tell the stories they want to tell. Recent films I liked: A Moment to Remember (2004), Island of Greed 黑金 (1997).
